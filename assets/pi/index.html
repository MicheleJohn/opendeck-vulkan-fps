When checked (Auto-detect ON): Automatically detects Steam, Proton, Lutris, Heroic & Gamescope games while ignoring browsers and desktop apps.
• To exclude a specific game: DISABLE_SD_FPS=1
When unchecked (Manual mode): Auto-detection is off. Enable only on chosen games using:
• Steam Launch Options: ENABLE_SD_FPS=1 %command%
• Lutris / Heroic Env: ENABLE_SD_FPS=1
